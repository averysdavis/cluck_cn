The problem with expiration dates 保bǎo质zhì期qī的de问wèn题tí

Food waste costs the meat industry an estimated 94 billion USD every year 食shí物wù浪làng费fèi每měi年nián给gěi肉ròu类lèi行háng业yè造zào成chéng估gū计jì940亿yì美měi元yuán的de损sǔn失shī。

Inaccurate expiration date labels often mislead consumers 不bù准zhǔn确què的de保bǎo质zhì期qī标biāo签qiān经jīng常cháng误wù导dǎo消xiāo费fèi者zhě。

Date labels are typically conservative estimates, which can lead to the unnecessary disposal of safe meat or the accidental consumption of spoiled meat 保bǎo质zhì期qī标biāo签qiān通tōng常cháng是shì保bǎo守shǒu的de估gū算suàn，这zhè可kě以yǐ导dǎo致zhì安ān全quán的de肉ròu被bèi不bù必bì要yào地de丢diū弃qì，或huò者zhě不bù小xiǎo心xīn吃chī到dào变biàn质zhì的de肉ròu。

Health Consequences of Unsafe Meat 不bù安ān全quán肉ròu类lèi的de健jiàn康kāng危wēi害hài

Consuming spoiled chicken can lead to infections from bacteria such as Salmonella and E. coli 食shí用yòng变biàn质zhì的de鸡jī肉ròu可kě能néng导dǎo致zhì沙shā门mén氏shì菌jūn与yǔ大dà肠cháng杆gǎn菌jūn等děng细xì菌jūn感gǎn染rǎn。

It is estimated that 1 in 9 people get sick after ingesting contaminated meat, with 1.52 million dying annually 据jù估gū计jì，每měi9人rén中zhōng就jiù有yǒu1人rén在zài食shí用yòng受shòu污wū染rǎn的de肉ròu类lèi后hòu生shēng病bìng，每měi年nián有yǒu152万wàn人rén因yīn此cǐ死sǐ亡wáng。

Environmental Impacts 对duì环huán境jìng的de影yǐng响xiǎng

Environmentally, the meat industry drives deforestation for grazing land, which in turn destroys habitats and reduces biodiversity 从cóng环huán境jìng角jiǎo度dù来lái看kàn，肉ròu类lèi产chǎn业yè为wèi了le获huò取qǔ放fàng牧mù用yòng地dì而ér推tuī动dòng森sēn林lín砍kǎn伐fá，这zhè反fǎn过guò来lái又yòu破pò坏huài了le栖qī息xī地dì，减jiǎn少shǎo了le生shēng物wù多duō样yàng性xìng。

Decomposing chicken releases methane (a greenhouse gas) 腐fǔ烂làn的de鸡jī肉ròu会huì释shì放fàng甲jiǎ烷wán（一yī种zhǒng温wēn室shì气qì体tǐ）。

Farms release harmful chemicals and waste products that pollute the atmosphere, waterways, and soil 农nóng场chǎng会huì释shì放fàng有yǒu害hài化huà学xué物wù质zhì和hé废fèi弃qì物wù，污wū染rǎn大dà气qì、水shuǐ道dào和hé土tǔ壤rǎng。

Our project 我wǒ们men的de项xiàng目mù

Our project is to engineer a biosensor to detect spoilage in meat as an alternative to traditional expiration dates 我wǒ们men的de项xiàng目mù是shì设shè计jì一yī种zhǒng生shēng物wù传chuán感gǎn器qì，用yòng于yú检jiǎn测cè肉ròu类lèi变biàn质zhì，作zuò为wéi传chuán统tǒng保bǎo质zhì期qī标biāo签qiān替tì代dài方fāng案àn。

The biosensor measures cadaverine, an organic compound produced as meat decomposes 该gāi生shēng物wù传chuán感gǎn器qì检jiǎn测cè尸shī胺àn，这zhè是shì一yī种zhǒng在zài肉ròu类lèi分fēn解jiě时shí产chǎn生shēng的de有yǒu机jī化huà合hé物wù。

As cadaverine levels rise, the biosensor responds by producing a blue chromoprotein, delivering a clear visual result 随suí着zhe尸shī胺àn水shuǐ平píng的de升shēng高gāo，该gāi生shēng物wù传chuán感gǎn器qì会huì通tōng过guò产chǎn生shēng蓝lán色sè色sè蛋dàn白bái来lái响xiǎng应yìng，呈chéng现xiàn出chū清qīng晰xī的de视shì觉jué结jié果guǒ。

In the future, we aim to adapt the biosensor for other meats, not just chicken 未wèi来lái，我wǒ们men的de目mù标biāo是shì将jiāng该gāi生shēng物wù传chuán感gǎn器qì推tuī广guǎng应yìng用yòng于yú其qí他tā肉ròu类lèi，而ér不bù仅jǐn仅jǐn是shì鸡jī肉ròu。

About the name 关guān于yú项xiàng目mù名míng称chēng

"The Cluck is Ticking" is a pun on "the clock is ticking," which means time is running out "The Cluck is Ticking" 是shì英yīng文wén短duǎn语yǔ "the clock is ticking"（意yì为wèi时shí间jiān紧jǐn迫pò）的de双shuāng关guān语yǔ。

The title swaps "clock" for "cluck," the sound a chicken makes, linking the urgency of food safety to our project's focus on chicken 标biāo题tí把bǎ "clock"（钟zhōng）换huàn成chéng了le "cluck"（鸡jī叫jiào声shēng），将jiāng食shí品pǐn安ān全quán的de紧jǐn迫pò性xìng与yǔ我wǒ们men项xiàng目mù对duì鸡jī肉ròu的de关guān注zhù联lián系xì了le起qǐ来lái。
