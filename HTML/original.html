The Cluck is Ticking

The problem with expiration dates 保质期的问题

Food waste costs the meat industry an estimated 94 billion USD every year 食物浪费每年给肉类行业造成估计940亿美元的损失。

Inaccurate expiration date labels often mislead consumers 不准确的保质期标签经常误导消费者。

Date labels are typically conservative estimates, which can lead to the unnecessary disposal of safe meat or the accidental consumption of spoiled meat 保质期标签通常是保守的估算，这可以导致安全的肉被不必要地丢弃，或者不小心吃到变质的肉。

Health Consequences of Unsafe Meat 不安全肉类的健康危害

Consuming spoiled chicken can lead to infections from bacteria such as Salmonella and E. coli 食用变质的鸡肉可能导致沙门氏菌与大肠杆菌等细菌感染。

It is estimated that 1 in 9 people get sick after ingesting contaminated meat, with 1.52 million dying annually 据估计，每9人中就有1人在食用受污染的肉类后生病，每年有152万人因此死亡。

Environmental Impacts 对环境的影响

Environmentally, the meat industry drives deforestation for grazing land, which in turn destroys habitats and reduces biodiversity 从环境角度来看，肉类产业为了获取放牧用地而推动森林砍伐，这反过来又破坏了栖息地，减少了生物多样性。

Decomposing chicken releases methane (a greenhouse gas) 腐烂的鸡肉会释放甲烷（一种温室气体）。

Farms release harmful chemicals and waste products that pollute the atmosphere, waterways, and soil 农场会释放有害化学物质和废弃物，污染大气、水道和土壤。

Our project 我们的项目

Our project is to engineer a biosensor to detect spoilage in meat as an alternative to traditional expiration dates 我们的项目是设计一种生物传感器，用于检测肉类变质，作为传统保质期标签替代方案。

The biosensor measures cadaverine, an organic compound produced as meat decomposes 该生物传感器检测尸胺，这是一种在肉类分解时产生的有机化合物。

As cadaverine levels rise, the biosensor responds by producing a blue chromoprotein, delivering a clear visual result 随着尸胺水平的升高，该生物传感器会通过产生蓝色色蛋白来响应，呈现出清晰的视觉结果。

In the future, we aim to adapt the biosensor for other meats, not just chicken 未来，我们的目标是将该生物传感器推广应用于其他肉类，而不仅仅是鸡肉。
